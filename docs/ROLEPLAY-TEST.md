# Role-play test: Yeti as Holly's client

Purpose: find every place a first-time or older client would get stuck, before a real client does. Yeti plays the client on his own phone. Holly runs her desk on hers. Nobody explains anything out loud that the screen should have explained.

## Setup (5 minutes, before the call)
- Holly signs in to her desk and opens Deals > New roadmap. She uses a real (closed) purchase agreement PDF if she has one, otherwise types two dates. Client name: Yeti. Client cell: Yeti's number. Lake home: on.
- Holly checks the dates against the contract, fixes any, then taps "Text Yeti the link".
- Yeti does NOT open the link yet.

## The run (play it straight, as a client who has never bought a house)
1. Yeti reads the text on his phone and opens the link. Say out loud what you think the page is for before scrolling.
2. Find: what happens next, when, and what you have to do. Time how long it takes to find each.
3. Add one date to your phone calendar from the page. Did it open the calendar? Did the event land on the right day?
4. Tap "I did this" on your own step. Did Holly's phone buzz? Did the page confirm it?
5. Ask a question in the box, something a nervous buyer asks ("do I have to be at the inspection?"). Holly answers from the desk. Did the reply arrive by text and show on the page?
6. Holly ticks the step done on the desk. Yeti reloads. Did the green tick and the next step make sense without being told?
7. Holly sends "Your offer, explained" from Stationery, pulling the names from the roadmap. Yeti receives it (text version, and the PDF by email or AirDrop). Read it as if you were 72 and this is your first sale. Mark every word you would have to ask about.
8. Holly sends "Closing day details". Same read. Did the wire-fraud warning register, or did it read like boilerplate?
9. Day-after check: did the reminder text arrive two days before the next step, and did it make sense on its own?

## What to write down (one line each, no fixing during the run)
| # | Where (page / letter / text) | What the client did or said | What they expected instead |
|---|---|---|---|

## Known limits to say out loud at the end, not during
- Letters email from the desk only once Holly's sending address is set up; until then it is Print / Save as PDF, or "Open in your email app".
- Documents are links on a step, not uploads.
- The lender and title company do not have their own login.

## After the run
- Every row in the table becomes a task on the board (Holly, Backlog) or a copy fix the same day.
- Words that stumped the client go into the glossary (TERMS in src/data/stationery.js) or get replaced in the step text (src/data/deal-templates.js).
