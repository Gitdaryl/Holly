# Questions for Holly - September launch

Ask in this order. Section 1 blocks the launch, section 2 blocks the seller
report, section 3 is the sales angle, section 4 is the baseline that lets us
prove the site worked. Keep it conversational, this is not a form.

---

## 1. Launch blockers

1. **Do you have listings right now, and how many?** How many did you list in
   the last 12 months?
2. **What is the address of the one you would put on the site first?** We want
   a real one live on day one, not a sample.
3. **Does Foundation Realty allow you a personal agent website?** Is there a
   required disclaimer, logo, or approval step before it goes live?
4. **What has to appear on every page for compliance?** Brokerage name, office
   address, Equal Housing logo, license number. Different brokerages differ and
   getting it wrong after launch is a bad look.
5. **Are you allowed to show other agents' listings** (an MLS/IDX feed), or only
   your own? This changes what the site can be.
6. **What email address should leads go to,** and what phone number do you want
   on the site? Confirm (517) 403-3413 is still right.
7. **Do you want the site at its own domain** (hollygriewahn.com or similar), or
   living under Foundation Realty's site?

## 2. Seller report

8. **When a listing goes live, what do you send the seller and how often?**
   Nothing, a phone call, a Zillow screenshot, something from the brokerage?
9. **What do sellers complain about most?** If the answer is "not hearing from
   you", the weekly report is the whole pitch.
10. **Would sellers be comfortable getting an automatic weekly email from you**
    with their listing's numbers? Any seller you would NOT send it to?
11. **For each active listing, what is the seller's first name and email,**
    and the date it went on the market? That is all the report needs.
12. **Do you want it sent automatically each Friday, or do you want to look at
    it and click send?** Right now it is built so you review first and nothing
    goes out until you press the button.

## 3. Winning more listings

13. **Where did your last 10 listings actually come from?** Referral, past
    client, sign call, Zillow, farming, walk-in. Be honest about the split.
14. **Which lakes do you actually sell on?** Not all 58. The 5 or 6 you want to
    own.
15. **What do you show a seller at a listing appointment today?** Printed CMA,
    iPad, brokerage deck, nothing?
16. **What is the objection you lose on?** Commission, "my nephew is an agent",
    a competitor's marketing, price expectations?
17. **Who is the agent you lose listings to,** and what do they do that you
    do not?
18. **Do you do anything at closing?** Gift, folder, follow-up. This is where
    referrals get made or missed.

## 4. Baseline so we can prove it worked

19. **Your numbers for the last 12 months:** listings taken, homes sold, average
    days on market, average sale price, list-to-sale price ratio.
20. **How many seller leads do you get in a normal month,** and what do you do
    with them?
21. **Do you have a Google Business Profile,** and how many reviews?
22. **What are you currently paying for** in marketing? Zillow, Realtor.com,
    postcards, brokerage fees?
23. **Do you have an email list of past clients?** How many, and where does it
    live?

---

### The photo question, ask it last

24. **Do you have professional photos of your current listings, and who owns
    them?** If the brokerage or a hired photographer owns the copyright, we
    cannot publish them without permission, no matter who paid.
