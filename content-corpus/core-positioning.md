# Holly Griewahn - Core Positioning

## Who She Is
Holly Griewahn is a licensed real estate agent with Foundation Realty, based in the Irish Hills area of southeast Michigan. She has specialized in lake property in Lenawee County for over 30 years.

## What Makes Her Different
- She knows 58 lakes personally - not as listings on a spreadsheet, but as places she has visited, walked, and watched change over decades
- She understands the nuances: which lakes allow jet skis, which have wake restrictions, which ones flood in spring, which have the best bass fishing, which have homeowners associations with strict rules
- She works with both buyers AND sellers - she understands both sides of every negotiation
- She is not chasing volume. She works with clients who value expertise over speed.
- She is the person her past clients call when their kids are ready to buy, or when they want to refer a friend

## Her Market
- Irish Hills Lakes - the constellation of 50+ lakes around the Irish Hills area of Lenawee County, Michigan
- Devils Lake (the largest and most well-known in the area)
- Cambridge Junction area
- US-12 corridor communities
- Neighboring lake communities extending into Hillsdale and Jackson counties

## Her Client
- Families looking for their first lake cottage or year-round lake home
- Retirees downsizing to a simpler life on the water
- Out-of-state buyers drawn to Michigan lakes from Ohio, Indiana, and greater metro Detroit
- Sellers who have owned for decades and want an agent who values their property's history

## Foundation Realty
Holly works with Foundation Realty, a local firm. She is not a big-box national franchise agent. That matters - she has no corporate quota pushing her toward volume over care.
