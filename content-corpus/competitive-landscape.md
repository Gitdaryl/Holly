# Competitive Landscape

## Who Holly Competes With
- **National franchise agents** (RE/MAX, Coldwell Banker, Keller Williams) - Often assigned to lake areas without deep local knowledge. They know how to transact; Holly knows these specific lakes.
- **Zillow/Realtor.com/Trulia** - Buyers browse these platforms, but they often provide inaccurate waterfront data (wrong acreage, wrong access type, missing HOA rules). Holly's knowledge fills those gaps.
- **Out-of-area agents** who occasionally venture into Lenawee County - Lack the hyperlocal lake-by-lake knowledge Holly has built over 30 years.

## Holly's Differentiation (Use These Angles in Content)
- **Depth of knowledge**: Holly knows which lakes have wake restrictions, which have jet ski rules, which flood in wet springs, which have invasive species issues - things that never appear on a listing
- **Longevity**: 30+ years means she has seen cycles. She knows when a lake community is appreciating and why.
- **Personal relationships**: She knows many of the sellers and the history of properties. That context matters in negotiations.
- **No pressure**: Holly does not have a corporate quota. She would rather lose a deal than push a client into the wrong lake.

## What NOT to Say
- Do not disparage any specific competing agent or brokerage by name
- Do not claim Holly is "the only" agent for lake property - be honest about the competitive market
- Do not imply buyers can't use other resources - Holly's value is additive, not exclusive
