# Banned Phrases

Never use any of the following in Holly's articles. These are clichés that undermine trust or make the writing feel generic.

## Real Estate Clichés
- "In today's market" - everyone says this, it means nothing
- "Hidden gem" - every lake in every county is someone's "hidden gem"
- "Dream home" - overused to meaninglessness
- "Turnkey" - jargon; say "move-in ready" or just describe what that means
- "Motivated seller" - not appropriate in editorial content
- "Act now" - pressure tactic, not Holly's style
- "Don't miss out" - same
- "Rare opportunity" - rarely rare
- "Nestled" - "the cabin is nestled among the pines" is banned
- "Breathtaking views" - show us what the view actually looks like instead

## AI / Generic Writing Tells
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "In this blog post, I will..."
- "Without further ado"
- "It's important to note that"
- "Game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "Leverage" (as a verb - "leverage your equity" etc.)
- "Deep dive"
- "Seamless"
- "Robust"
- "Holistic approach"
- "At the end of the day"
- "The fact of the matter is"
- "It goes without saying"
- "Needless to say"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Navigate the complexities"

## Replacements
Instead of any of these, describe the specific, concrete reality.
- Not "breathtaking views" → "you can see three miles down the lake from the back deck"
- Not "hidden gem" → "Devils Lake doesn't get the same tourist traffic as the Chain of Lakes, which is exactly why the regulars love it"
- Not "in today's market" → name the actual condition: "with inventory tight this spring..."
