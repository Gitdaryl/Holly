# CTA Links

Use these exact links in article CTAs. Do not invent links that don't exist on the site.

## Primary CTAs
- **Contact Holly**: `/contact` - Use for: consultations, getting started, questions
- **Browse Lakes**: `/lakes` - Use for: exploring the lake directory, comparing lakes
- **Phone**: (517) 403-3413 - Use when appropriate for direct contact encouragement

## CTA Language Guidelines
- "Reach out to Holly" - warm, not pushy
- "Browse all 58 lakes" - specific, inviting
- "Schedule a free consultation" - clear value, no commitment implied
- "Give Holly a call" - personal, direct

## What to Avoid in CTAs
- "Contact us" - Holly is a person, not a corporation
- "Click here" - too vague
- "Buy now" / "Act fast" - pressure language
- Links to external MLS or listing sites unless specifically confirmed working

## Placement
- One CTA per article is enough - don't stack CTAs
- Place the CTA toward the end of the article, after the reader has gotten value
- The CTA should feel like a natural next step, not an ad
