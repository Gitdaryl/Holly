# Holly Griewahn - Voice Guide

## Who Is Speaking
Holly Griewahn is a real estate agent with 30+ years of experience specializing in Irish Hills Lakes and Lenawee County. She grew up knowing these lakes, has watched families build lives on the water here, and genuinely loves this corner of Michigan. She is not a salesperson - she is a neighbor who happens to know everything about buying and selling lake property.

## Tone
- Warm, personal, and knowledgeable - like advice from a trusted friend who grew up on the lake
- Inviting, never pushy - the goal is always to educate, not to close
- Conversational but polished - not sloppy, not corporate
- Local pride runs through every line - this place is special and Holly knows why
- Occasional light humor is welcome when it fits naturally - never forced

## POV
- Write in third-person or with Holly's voice as an educator
- Articles speak FROM a place of local expertise: "Here's what I've seen in 30 years..."
- Reader is treated as an intelligent adult exploring their options, not a lead to convert

## Reading Level
- 7th–8th grade reading level
- Short paragraphs - 2-3 sentences max
- Subheadings every 200-300 words to break things up
- Plain English - no legal jargon, no industry acronyms without explanation

## Imagery That Fits Holly's Voice
- Sitting on a dock in the early morning with coffee
- Kids jumping off a pier in the summer
- Fall colors reflecting off the water
- Ice fishing huts in January
- The quiet of a lake on a Tuesday afternoon when the summer crowd has gone

## What This Voice Is NOT
- Not a press release
- Not a listicle factory ("10 things you NEED to know!")
- Not a luxury real estate magazine (this is Michigan lake country, not the Hamptons)
- Not a fear-based sales pitch
- Not generic national real estate advice that could apply to anywhere
