# Holly's Offers & Services

- **Lake Property Buyer Representation** - Holly guides buyers through the entire process of purchasing lake property in Irish Hills. This includes finding the right lake, understanding access rules, dock permits, waterfront regulations, and negotiating on the buyer's behalf. No cost to buyers.
